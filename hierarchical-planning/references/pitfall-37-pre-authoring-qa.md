# Pitfall 37 - Pre-Authoring Design Q&A Prevents Plan Rewrites

When to use: the source material (external report, competitor analysis) implies design decisions with meaningful trade-offs.
Lesson: present the decisions as a structured Q&A BEFORE authoring; authoring first means rewriting after the answers change the tree.

When the source material implies design decisions with meaningful trade-offs, present them as a structured Q&A BEFORE authoring any plan documents. The user's answers change leaf sizing, tree structure, and scope - authoring first means rewriting.

## Format

10 or fewer questions per batch, each with:

- **Q#** - one-line title
- **Rec** - your recommendation with rationale (the user wants you to extrapolate, not just ask)
- **Impact** - what changes if the decision goes the other way

Wait for ALL answers before writing any .md files.

## Distinction

This runs BEFORE authoring. Step 6.8 (open-questions walkthrough, `references/open-questions-walkthrough.md`) resolves questions AFTER authoring. Use both: pre-authoring Q&A for fork-in-the-road decisions, post-authoring walkthrough for the tree's residual OPEN-QUESTIONS.md.

Worked example: one parity session asked 7 questions before authoring 14 documents - zero rewrites needed.
